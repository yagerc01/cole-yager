About

I'm a Senior Technical Program Manager with 10+ years of experience delivering mission-critical systems across defense, government, and hyperscale commercial environments. I specialize in aligning engineering, product, and infrastructure teams around clear technical strategy, roadmaps, and metrics — turning complex dependencies into organized, executable programs.

My background spans nuclear submarine operations and naval intelligence, federal government IT modernization at the NGA and IRS, and most recently large-scale ad-serving systems at Amazon. Across all of it, the common thread has been making hard things move: managing cross-functional delivery, driving clarity under ambiguity, and keeping execution grounded in data.

I'm currently completing my MBA with an AI Certificate at George Washington University while working full-time at Amazon — because apparently I enjoy being busy.
